## Job Market Paper
**When Colleges Come Closer: The Effects of Local Higher Education Expansion on Educational Outcomes in India**

This paper evaluates the impact of Model Degree Colleges established under India's Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan. RUSA prioritized educationally backward districts with a low College Population Index, a low female Gross Enrollment Ratio, and a high SC/ST population. Using PLFS data from 2017 to 2023 and exploiting variation in college-opening years across districts, I estimate the effect of increased local college access on education and labor market outcomes using a staggered difference-in-differences design. The results show that opening a Model Degree College increases completed years of schooling by 0.84 years, with effects higher among boys than girls of the targeted population in these underserved districts. The paper contributes to understanding the importance of reducing the distance to college, which could translate into a higher demographic dividend.

## Work in Progress

**Impact of Sexual and Reproductive Health Education on Adolescent Well-being**

This paper evaluates the causal impact of the Peer Educator initiative under the Rashtriya Kishor Swasthya Karyakram (RKSK) on adolescents' knowledge of HIV, teenage pregnancy, and attitudes toward gender-based violence in India. Implemented in selected High Priority Districts (HPDs) in each state, the program enables a quasi-experimental analysis using a difference-in-differences framework. The results indicate that the program significantly increased HIV awareness by 11.7 percentage points and the use of modern contraception by 2.33 percentage points. However, it did not lead to significant improvements in comprehensive knowledge of HIV or attitudes toward gender-based violence.

## Other Papers

**National Lok Adalat: Solution for Clearing Backlogs in Subordinate Courts of India?** — Master's Paper, McGill University, 2018
*Advisor: Prof. Matthieu Chemin*

This paper investigates the effectiveness of the Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) mechanism in reducing the backlogs of a formal legal system. A National Lok Adalat was conducted on 14th July 2018 all over India except in the state of Gujarat due to some exogenous shock (festival of Rathyatra). Gujarat conducted the National Lok Adalat on 22nd July 2018. This difference in the day on which the Lok Adalat is conducted is used to estimate the effectiveness of the National Lok Adalat. Using the National Judicial Data Grid (NJDG) daily data, I found that the national Lok Adalat was effective in reducing the total number of pending cases registered in India's subordinate courts.
