## Job Market Paper
**When Colleges Come Closer: The Effects of Local Higher Education Expansion on Educational Outcomes in India**

This paper evaluates the impact of Model Degree Colleges established under India's Rashtriya Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan. RUSA prioritized educationally backward districts with a low College Population Index, a low female Gross Enrollment Ratio, and a high SC/ST population. Using PLFS data from 2017 to 2023 and exploiting variation in college-opening years across districts, I estimate the effect of increased local college access on education and labor market outcomes using a staggered difference-in-differences design. The results show that opening a Model Degree College increases completed years of schooling by 0.84 years, with effects higher among boys than girls of the targeted population in these underserved districts. The paper contributes to understanding the importance of reducing the distance to college, which could translate into a higher demographic dividend.

**Impact of Sexual and Reproductive Health Education on Adolescent Well-being**
Evaluates the causal impact of the Peer Educator initiative under the Rashtriya Kishor Swasthya Karyakram (RKSK) on adolescents' knowledge of HIV, teenage pregnancy, and attitudes toward gender-based violence in India, using a difference-in-differences framework.
